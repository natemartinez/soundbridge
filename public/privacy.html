SoundBridge Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 21, 2026

SoundBridge ("we", "our", or "us") connects musicians with churches and organizations that need live music. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information. By using the SoundBridge mobile application, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account

When you register, we collect your email address, display name, role (musician or church/organization), and the password you create (stored securely via Firebase Authentication — we never see your raw password).

Profile

To help match musicians with gigs, we collect optional profile information including your city and state, bio, profile photo, instruments played, and — for organizations — church or venue details. This information is visible to other users.

Payments

Gig payments and premium subscription charges are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not store your card number, CVV, or full payment details on our servers. We store only a Stripe customer ID (a token) and a record of completed transactions (amount, gig reference, timestamp) for accounting and dispute resolution purposes.

Audio & Microphone

SoundBridge requests access to your device's microphone to support audio-related features. We do not record or transmit audio to our servers without your explicit action.

Messages

Messages exchanged between users within the app are stored in our database to deliver them to recipients and display conversation history. We do not sell or share message content with third parties.

Usage Data

We collect standard technical information including device type, operating system version, app version, and crash reports. This data is used solely to diagnose issues and improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To display your profile to other users for gig matching
  • To process payments for gigs and premium subscriptions
  • To deliver in-app messages between musicians and organizations
  • To send transactional notifications (gig confirmations, payment receipts)
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not use your information to serve third-party advertisements.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Other users: Your public profile (name, location, instruments, bio) is visible to other registered users to facilitate gig connections.
  • Stripe: Payment details are shared with Stripe to process transactions. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Google / Firebase: Our backend infrastructure runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions). Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your profile and personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain records for legal or financial compliance (e.g., payment transaction records, which we retain for 7 years).

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase security rules that restrict data access to authenticated users, and Stripe's PCI-compliant payment processing. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Children's Privacy

SoundBridge is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: You can view and edit your profile information at any time within the app.
  • Account deletion: You may request deletion of your account by contacting us at the email below. We will process your request within 30 days.
  • Microphone: You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings. This may limit certain app features.
  • Notifications: You can manage push notification preferences in your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
