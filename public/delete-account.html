Delete Your SoundBridge Account

Request permanent deletion of your account and personal data

You can request deletion of your SoundBridge account and all associated personal data at any time by following the steps below. Requests are processed within 30 days.

How to Request Account Deletion

1
Send a deletion request email Email us from the address associated with your SoundBridge account. This lets us verify your identity before deleting any data.
2
Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request" Include your display name and the email address you registered with so we can locate your account quickly.
3
We'll confirm and process your request You'll receive a confirmation email within 2 business days. Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request.
Send Deletion Request →

What Gets Deleted

Data type Action Timeline
Account & profile (name, bio, photo, instruments, location) Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Authentication credentials (email & password) Permanently deleted Within 30 days
In-app messages Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Gig applications Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Stripe customer record Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Payment transaction records (amount, date, gig reference) Retained 7 years (legal/tax compliance)
⚠️ Payment transaction records (not card details) are retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations. These records contain no payment card information — only transaction amounts, dates, and internal references.
